3. Form rules

  • Four parts. The progress bar and the "Part X of 4" label update on every step.
  • Part 1 is three fields. Name and phone prefill from the VIP checkout and stay editable. Handle is optional and always starts blank. There is no registration survey feeding this page, so nothing else is known about them before they fill this in.
  • Part 3 photos: minimum 5, maximum 10. JPG, PNG, HEIC. They cannot continue under 5.
  • Part 3 videos: "Nope, I'm completely starting from zero" is exclusive. Selecting it clears every other option and hides the upload block. Selecting any other option clears it and reveals the upload block.
  • Part 4 caps: personality is max 3, brand types is max 5. Once the cap is hit the remaining options grey out and the counter turns.
  • Back button is hidden on Part 1. Continue becomes Submit on Part 4.
  • On submit, show the "That's everything" state in place of the form. Do not redirect them off the page, the bonus course and success centre are further down.

4. What fires on submit

  1. Write every answer and every uploaded file back to the contact record.
  2. Tag the contact so the portfolio build workflow starts.
  3. Notify the fulfilment team that a new build is in the queue.
  4. Timestamp the submission. The promise on the page is most portfolios back in about 20 minutes and 24 hours maximum, so the clock needs to start here.

5. Before you hand it back

  • Test on a phone first. Most VIP buyers open this on mobile.
  • Load the page as a real contact and confirm no raw {{token}} text is visible anywhere.
  • Click all three calendar buttons and confirm the same event lands.
  • Submit the form end to end with real photos and confirm they arrive on the contact record.
  • Confirm the BUILD NOTES section is deleted.
