04 My story

Four thousand attics in Phoenix heat

I have been an HVAC technician in Phoenix for twelve years and I run my own two van outfit now. Most of my calls are houses, which means attics, in summers that sit at 110 outside and a lot worse up there.

Somewhere around four thousand attics in, I can usually tell what is wrong with a system from the sound it makes before I open anything. When I am not working I am at little league with my three kids, on the desert trails early on Sunday before the heat comes back, or standing over a smoker in the backyard taking brisket far too seriously.

I have never made anything for a brand. I have spent twelve years explaining expensive problems to people in their own driveway, which is close to the same job.

Send me the product and the brief.
